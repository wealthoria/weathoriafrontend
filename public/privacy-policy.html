Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Wealthoria Education Private Limited ("Wealthoria", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website www.wealthoria.in or subscribe to our membership and educational services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when registering, making a payment, or contacting our team:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, and email address provided during membership signup or book pre-orders.
  • Payment & Transaction Details: Subscription plan, payment IDs, mandate registration status, and transaction timestamps processed through Razorpay. We do not store raw card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Usage & Portal Data: Login timestamps, device identifiers, and educational module progress within the Member Portal.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for educational and administrative purposes:

  • To manage and deliver your ₹99/month Wealthoria Membership and portal access.
  • To communicate essential updates regarding webinars, weekly market research, order filings, and video releases.
  • To handle customer support inquiries and provide assistance for login, payment verification, and billing issues.
  • To fulfill physical book orders and deliver study materials across Karnataka and India.
  • To comply with statutory, financial, and tax obligations under applicable Indian laws.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate our platform:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay Software Ltd. for secure UPI, card, and mandate processing.
  • Infrastructure: Firebase (Google Cloud) for secure member authentication and database hosting.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable laws, regulatory directives, or law enforcement authorities.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL (HTTPS), and access controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal account information at any time by contacting our support team.
