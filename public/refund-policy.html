Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Wealthoria Education Private Limited ("Wealthoria"), we are committed to transparent and fair business practices. This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms governing subscriptions, publications, and educational services purchased on www.wealthoria.in.

1. Monthly Membership Subscriptions (₹99/month)

Wealthoria provides instant digital access to member-only educational lessons, 44 financial calculators in the Fundamental Analysis Lab, weekly market filings, and research reports.

  • Cancellation: You can cancel your monthly recurring auto-debit subscription at any time with no lock-in period and no cancellation fee.
  • Access After Cancellation: Once cancelled, you will not be billed for any subsequent cycles, and you will retain full access to all member features until the expiration date of your current billing period.
  • Refunds: Because access to digital proprietary tools and weekly content is granted immediately upon successful activation, monthly subscription fees already charged are generally non-refundable once a cycle has commenced.
  • Duplicate Charges: In the event of a technical glitch causing duplicate deductions for the same billing cycle, the duplicate amount will be refunded in full upon verification.

2. Physical Book Pre-Orders (ಹೂಡಿಕೆಯ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ)

Wealthoria offers pre-orders for its comprehensive financial education book written in Kannada (₹1,499).

  • Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: You may cancel your pre-order at any time before the book is dispatched from our fulfillment facility for a 100% full refund.
  • Damaged or Defective Delivery: If you receive a book that has printing defects or was damaged during transit, notify us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We will dispatch a replacement copy at no additional charge or issue a full refund upon return of the damaged copy.

3. Special Workshops & Masterclasses

For ticketed live webinars or specialized seminars:

  • Cancellations requested at least 48 hours prior to the event schedule will receive a full refund.
  • Cancellations requested within 48 hours of the event are non-refundable, but access to session recordings and digital learning handouts will be provided.
  • If an event is rescheduled or cancelled by Wealthoria, participants will receive a full 100% refund or free transfer to the rescheduled date.

4. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed through our payment gateway partner, Razorpay Software Ltd., and credited back to the original payment source (UPI account, debit card, or credit card) within 5 to 7 business days as per banking turnaround standards.

5. How to Request Cancellation or Refund

To request a cancellation, refund, or check the status of your payment, please contact our support desk:

Email: support@wealthoria.in
Phone: +91 90197 59001 (Mon – Sat, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM IST)
Details to Include: Your registered email ID, mobile number, and Razorpay payment ID (e.g., `pay_...`).
