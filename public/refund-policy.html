Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for subscribing to Wealthoria. This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines the terms regarding our ₹99 monthly membership subscriptions, automated renewal debits, and educational products.

1. Membership Subscription (₹99 / month)

Wealthoria offers access to an exclusive Member Portal and dashboard at ₹99 per month. Active subscribers receive access to curated financial video lessons, weekly market and order win summaries, Kannada learning articles, and 44 Fundamental Analysis Lab valuation calculators.

2. Subscription Renewal and Auto-Debit

Your subscription renews automatically every 30 days based on your authorized payment method (UPI AutoPay or Card mandate). This ensures continuous access without manual monthly payments.

  • You can cancel your recurring auto-debit authorization at any time directly through your dashboard settings or by writing to support@wealthoria.in.
  • When you cancel auto-debit, no future debits will occur. You will continue to have full access to the member portal until the end of your currently paid 30-day period.

3. Access After Expiry or Failed Renewal

Once your paid subscription cycle expires, if an automatic renewal payment fails or has been cancelled, member portal access is deactivated. You will not be charged extra, and you can renew at any time.

4. Reactivating Your Membership

You can reactivate your membership at any time by visiting www.wealthoria.in/subscription.html and entering your registered email address. Once payment is confirmed, your account access is instantly restored.

5. Refund Eligibility

Due to the immediate access granted to proprietary digital educational materials, calculators, and videos upon payment:

  • Monthly subscription fees (₹99) are generally non-refundable once the billing cycle has commenced.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Charges: If you were accidentally charged twice for the same billing cycle due to a technical glitch, please notify us within 7 days. Verified duplicate charges will be refunded in full to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

6. Book Pre-Orders Cancellation

For physical pre-orders of "ಹೂಡಿಕೆಯ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ" (The Science of Investing), cancellation requests received prior to order dispatch will be eligible for a full refund. Once the book has been packed and shipped, standard return policies apply.
