1. Nature and Purpose of Our Content

Wealthoria Education Private Limited is committed to providing responsible financial education and investor awareness content while adhering to applicable laws, regulations, circulars and guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

Our primary objective is to provide general financial-market education, financial literacy and investor awareness. Our educational content may include, among other subjects:

  • Stock-market and investment concepts
  • Fundamental analysis and financial-statement analysis
  • Business-model, industry and sector analysis
  • Economic and market concepts
  • Valuation methodologies
  • Risk-management concepts
  • Asset allocation and diversification
  • Behavioural finance and investment psychology
  • Portfolio-construction concepts
  • Technical-analysis concepts for educational purposes
  • Corporate announcements and financial results explained from an educational perspective
  • Historical market events and case studies
  • Government policies and their potential impact on industries
  • General concepts relating to equities, mutual funds, bonds and other financial-market instruments
  • Methods and frameworks investors can use to conduct their own analysis and due diligence

The purpose of such content is to educate and improve financial-market understanding and analytical capability.

2. Educational Content Is Not Investment Advice

Our educational content is intended to provide general information and education and should not be construed as personalised investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security.

Unless expressly provided by an appropriately registered and authorised entity/person under the applicable regulatory framework, our educational content does not constitute:

  • Investment advice or personalised investment advice
  • A research recommendation
  • A trading call
  • A buy, sell or hold recommendation
  • A portfolio-specific recommendation
  • An entry or exit recommendation
  • A target-price or stop-loss recommendation
  • A solicitation or offer to transact in securities
  • Any assurance or guarantee of returns or profits

We do not make investment decisions on behalf of viewers, readers, students or participants.

3. Education Versus Investment Recommendation

We recognise the distinction between genuine investor education and activities that may constitute investment advice or recommendation under the applicable SEBI regulatory framework.

Our educational content is structured with the objective of explaining concepts, analytical methodologies, frameworks, financial information and investment principles, rather than providing unauthorised personalised investment recommendations.

Educational explanations may discuss how an investor can evaluate revenue growth, profitability, ROE and ROCE, cash flows, debt, working capital, competitive advantages, management quality, capital allocation, industry structure and valuation methodologies. Such educational discussion should not be interpreted as a direction or instruction to invest in any particular security.

4. Discussion of Companies and Securities

From time to time, our educational content may refer to or discuss specific companies, securities, sectors, indices or financial instruments for educational illustration, historical case studies, explaining analytical concepts, understanding business models, analysing financial statements, understanding industry developments, explaining corporate events or demonstrating analytical methodologies.

The mention, discussion or analysis of a company or security does not, by itself, constitute a recommendation to buy, sell or hold that security. Viewers and readers should independently evaluate the relevant security and should not make investment decisions solely on the basis of our educational content.

5. Market Price and Market Data

We recognise that SEBI has prescribed specific requirements concerning the sharing and usage of securities-market price data for educational purposes.

We endeavour to comply with the applicable requirements relating to market-price data, market information, charts and other securities-market data, including the requirements under SEBI Circular No. HO/47/17/12(11)2025-MRD-POD3/I/11107/2026 dated 8 May 2026 – Norms for sharing and usage of price data for educational purposes, including the applicable requirements effective from 1 July 2026.

Market data used in our educational content is therefore subject to the applicable regulatory requirements and permitted use conditions.

6. No Guarantee or Assurance of Returns

We do not guarantee, assure or promise any specific return, profit, capital appreciation, future price, investment performance or financial outcome.

Any historical return, performance figure, hypothetical calculation, illustration, back-tested result or historical example presented in our educational content is provided solely for educational and analytical purposes.

Past performance is not indicative of future results. Securities-market investments are subject to market risks, including the possible loss of part or all of the principal invested.

7. Historical and Hypothetical Examples

Historical companies, securities, prices, returns, charts, financial results and market events may be used to explain investment concepts. Similarly, hypothetical examples may be used to demonstrate compounding, valuation, asset allocation, risk, portfolio construction, return calculations and financial planning.

Such examples are illustrative and educational only and should not be interpreted as forecasts, guarantees or recommendations.

8. No Personalised Investment Recommendations

Our general educational content does not take into consideration the individual circumstances of each viewer or reader, including income, existing assets and liabilities, investment objectives, risk tolerance, investment horizon, liquidity requirements, tax circumstances or individual financial situation.

Accordingly, no viewer or reader should treat general educational content as personalised investment advice.

Individuals seeking personalised investment advice should consult an appropriately SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.

9. Independent Research and Due Diligence

We encourage investors to undertake appropriate independent research and due diligence before making any investment decision.

Such independent assessment should ordinarily include, among other factors, consideration of the following:

  • Financial position
  • Business fundamentals
  • Valuation
  • Risk factors
  • Management quality
  • Industry conditions
  • Regulatory developments
  • Investment horizon
  • Individual risk profile

No investment decision should be made solely on the basis of information published by Wealthoria Education Private Limited.

10. Educational Courses and Workshops

Our courses, workshops, webinars and educational programs are designed to provide financial-market education and analytical knowledge. Educational subjects may include fundamental analysis, technical-analysis concepts, financial-statement analysis, business analysis, valuation, risk management, asset allocation, mutual-fund concepts, investment psychology and other financial-market concepts.

Participation in an educational program does not create an adviser-client relationship unless such relationship is separately established through an appropriately regulated and authorised arrangement.

11. Social Media and Digital Platforms

The same principles apply to content published through YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X and other social-media platforms, websites, blogs, podcasts, webinars, online communities, messaging platforms and other digital communication channels.

Changing the platform does not change the nature or regulatory requirements applicable to the underlying communication. We endeavour to ensure that our digital content remains within the applicable regulatory framework governing educational activities.

12. Referral, Affiliate and Commercial Relationships

Where applicable, we may have commercial, referral, affiliate or other business relationships with third-party platforms or service providers.

Any such relationship will not alter the educational nature of our content. Where a disclosure is required in relation to a particular commercial relationship, sponsorship, paid promotion, referral arrangement or other material connection, appropriate disclosure will be provided in accordance with applicable requirements.

Commercial relationships should not be interpreted as an endorsement or recommendation of any particular financial product or security unless expressly stated by an appropriately authorised and regulated entity.

13. SEBI Regulatory Framework

Our educational-content practices are designed with reference to applicable SEBI regulations, circulars and guidelines, including, as applicable:

A. SEBI Circular dated 29 January 2025

Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/11 relating to provisions concerning association of persons regulated by SEBI, Market Infrastructure Institutions and their agents with persons engaged in prohibited activities. This framework is relevant to distinguishing legitimate investor education from activities involving unauthorised advice/recommendations and unpermitted claims relating to returns or performance.

B. SEBI Circular dated 8 May 2026

Circular No. HO/47/17/12(11)2025-MRD-POD3/I/11107/2026 – Norms for sharing and usage of price data for educational purposes. This framework governs the sharing and usage of securities-market price data for educational purposes, including the applicable requirements effective from 1 July 2026.

C. SEBI Circular dated 26 February 2026

Circular No. HO/(79)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC – Ease of Doing Investment (EoDI): Disclosure of registered name and registration number by SEBI-regulated entities and their agents on Social Media Platforms (SMPs). Where applicable to a SEBI-regulated entity or person, the prescribed social-media disclosure requirements shall be followed.

14. Important Regulatory Limitation

Regulatory requirements may change from time to time. Accordingly, this disclosure is based on the regulatory framework applicable as of the effective date stated above and may be amended when applicable laws, regulations, circulars, guidelines or regulatory interpretations change.

The existence of this disclosure should not be interpreted as a representation that any particular content, communication or activity has been approved, endorsed, certified or registered by SEBI.

15. Investor Awareness Statement

Wealthoria Education Private Limited believes that responsible financial education should help investors understand how to think about investments, rather than simply tell them what to buy or sell.

Our objective is to promote: Financial literacy → Analytical thinking → Risk awareness → Independent decision-making.

We encourage investors to understand the risks associated with securities-market investments and to seek professional advice from appropriately regulated professionals whenever personalised advice is required.
