Take a guess

Three questions. Does your answer match the data?

Three of the end-of-chapter questions from the book. Pick an answer and see what the book says.

Question 1  ·  Chapter 3

Your stock has moved between ₹410 and ₹440 for five months. Today it touched ₹446 during the day. Should you buy right now?

Right. That's the book's answer too.The book's answer is different.

Not yet. A touch during the day doesn't count; the close does. If price closes above ₹440, that's a breakout. In Reliance, a two-and-a-half-month range ended on the day it closed above ₹1,432.

Question 2  ·  Chapter 4

A zone has held price five times. Is it even stronger the sixth time?

Right. That's the book's answer too.The book's answer is different.

No. Every test uses up the orders waiting there. The more often a zone is tested, the more likely it is to break. ITC's zone held three times and broke on the fourth.

Question 3  ·  Chapter 11

Three days after you bought, price closed back inside the pattern. Your stop loss is still far away. What should you do?

Right. That's the book's answer too.The book's answer is different.

Get out. That trade is over. A failed pattern runs fast the other way, by more than 9% about half the time.
